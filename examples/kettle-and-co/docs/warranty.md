# Kettle & Co — Warranty and Repair Policy

## Warranty Coverage Periods

Kettle & Co stands behind the craftsmanship of every item we make. All products are covered against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship from the date of delivery:

- **Stovetop and Electric Kettles & Teapots:** 1-year comprehensive warranty (including heating elements, thermostats, welds, and handles).
- **Cast Iron Cookware (Dutch Ovens and Skillets):** 2-year structural and enamel warranty against cracking, warping, or factory enamel defects.
- **Tea Accessories (Trays and Infusers):** 6-month warranty against structural splits or mesh detachment.

## Repair or Replacement Resolution

If your product develops a covered fault within the warranty period, even after the 30-day return window has passed, Kettle & Co will either **repair the item free of charge** at our service workshop or **send a brand-new replacement unit**, including free two-way courier pickup and delivery via Delhivery or BlueDart. If the exact model is out of stock, we offer an equivalent upgrade or full store credit.

## What Is Not Covered

Our warranty covers normal household use and does not apply to:

- Cosmetic discoloration, mineral limescale build-up, or surface patina from regular cooking.
- Rust on bare cast iron resulting from soaking in water, dishwasher use, or lack of oil seasoning.
- Accidental breakage of borosilicate glass or damage caused by overheating an empty kettle (boil-dry on gas stoves).
- Commercial restaurant or catering use.

## How to Claim Warranty Support

To start a warranty repair or replacement claim, call Kettle & Co customer support with your Order ID and registered phone number or email. Our agent will verify your order, record a support ticket with the defect details, and arrange courier pickup within 2 business days.
