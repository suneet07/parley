# Kettle & Co — Frequently Asked Questions

## Support Hours and Contact

Kettle & Co customer support is available seven days a week from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST (Asia/Kolkata). You can reach our phone support line during operating hours for help with order tracking, returns, refunds, warranty claims, or product recommendations. Outside of operating hours, or if all specialists are busy, we can create a support ticket or book a callback at a time convenient for you. You can also reach our support team by email at support@kettleandco.example.com.

## Payment Methods and Cash on Delivery (COD)

We accept all major Indian payment methods, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Visa, Mastercard, RuPay debit and credit cards, and net banking. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available across most serviceable pin codes in India for orders up to ₹10,000 at no extra fee. For COD returns, refunds are processed via UPI or bank transfer once the returned item passes inspection, or issued as instant store credit.

## Order Modifications and Cancellations

Orders in the `processing` stage can be modified or cancelled before they are handed over to our courier partners. Once an order has been marked as `shipped` with Delhivery or BlueDart, it cannot be cancelled in transit, though you may initiate a free return within 30 days after delivery.

## Account and Identity Verification

To protect your privacy and order security, our phone support agent will ask for your Order ID (for example, `KC-10482`) along with the phone number or email address used when placing the order before sharing order details or making changes such as returns, refunds, or store credit.

## Bulk and Gifting Orders

We offer custom gift wrapping and corporate bulk orders on our kettles, teapots, and cast iron cookware. Please ask our support team to create a ticket with your required quantities and delivery timeline, and our gifting team will respond within one business day.
