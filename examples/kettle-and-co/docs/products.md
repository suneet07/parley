# Kettle & Co — Product Catalog and Care Guide

## Classic Steel Kettle 1.5L (`KTL-STL-15` — ₹2,499)

Crafted from food-grade 304 stainless steel with a triple-layer encapsulated base for rapid, even heating on gas, induction, and ceramic cooktops. Features a cool-touch ergonomic Bakelite handle and a gentle whistle cap. **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water; descale monthly using a 1:3 solution of white vinegar and water.

## Copper Pour-Over Kettle (`KTL-CPR-10` — ₹3,999)

Designed for specialty coffee and artisanal tea brewing, this 1.0L precision gooseneck kettle features a solid copper-plated stainless steel body with a balanced Rosewood handle for steady pour control. **Care:** Hand-wash only with a soft sponge; avoid abrasive pads to preserve the copper finish.

## TempControl Gooseneck Electric Kettle (`KTL-ELC-17` — ₹4,599)

A 1200W variable-temperature electric kettle (40°C to 100°C in 1°C steps) with a 60-minute keep-warm hold function, built-in brew stopwatch, and boil-dry protection. **Care:** Wipe the power base with a dry cloth and descale the interior regularly.

## Borosilicate Glass Teapot 1.2L (`KTL-GLS-12` — ₹1,899)

Hand-blown thermal-shock-resistant borosilicate glass teapot with a removable stainless steel loose-leaf infuser basket. Safe for direct low-flame gas use and dishwasher top-rack cleaning.

## Cast Iron Dutch Oven 6L (`CI-DOV-60` — ₹7,800)

Heavy-duty heirloom cast iron Dutch oven with a chip-resistant matte interior enamel and self-basting condensation lid spikes. Ideal for slow-cooked curries, biryanis, sourdough baking, and braising; oven-safe up to 260°C. **Care:** Allow to cool before washing; use wooden or silicone utensils.

## Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron Skillet 26cm (`CI-SKL-26` — ₹2,999)

Foundry-seasoned with cold-pressed flaxseed oil for a natural non-stick patina that improves with every cook. **Care:** Rinse with warm water, dry thoroughly on the stove, and rub a thin coat of cooking oil after each use. Do not soak or put in the dishwasher.

## Bamboo Tea Tray (`ACC-BMB-TRY` — ₹899) & Brass-Rim Infuser (`ACC-INF-SS` — ₹499)

Our sustainably harvested Moso bamboo serving tray and ultra-fine 304 stainless steel tea infuser with a hammered brass rim complete the daily tea ritual. Wipe bamboo dry after use.
