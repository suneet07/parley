# Kettle & Co — Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Charges

Kettle & Co offers free standard shipping across India on all orders above ₹999. For orders totaling ₹999 or below, a flat shipping fee of ₹79 is applied at checkout. All prices on our store are listed in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable GST.

## Dispatch and Delivery Timelines

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are typically dispatched within 24 hours from our Bengaluru or Delhi fulfilment centres. Estimated transit times after dispatch are:

- **Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune):** 3 to 5 business days.
- **Non-metro cities and Tier-2 / Tier-3 towns:** 5 to 8 business days.

Cast iron cookware items are double-boxed with reinforced corner protection, which may add one business day to preparation during peak festival sales.

## Courier Partners and Order Tracking

We ship exclusively with trusted national courier partners **Delhivery** and **BlueDart**. As soon as your order leaves our warehouse, its status updates to `shipped` and you receive an SMS and email with the carrier name, tracking number (such as `BD7781234` or `DL9901482`), and estimated delivery date. You can also call our support line anytime and ask to track your shipment after verifying your order ID and phone number or email.

## Delayed or Missed Deliveries

If your shipment is delayed past its estimated delivery date or marked undeliverable after a missed delivery attempt, our courier partner will automatically re-attempt delivery on the next business day. Our support team can also log a priority carrier escalation ticket or schedule a callback to keep you updated.
