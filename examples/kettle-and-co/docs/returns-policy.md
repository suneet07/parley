# Kettle & Co — Returns and Refunds Policy

## 30-Day Return Window

Kettle & Co offers a hassle-free 30-day return policy on all delivered orders. You may start a return within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery (`days_since_delivery <= 30`). Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories, lids, infusers, and instruction booklets. Orders that are still processing or in transit must be delivered before a return pickup can be scheduled.

## Free Reverse Pickup

Every eligible return includes free reverse pickup from your delivery address. Once your return is confirmed, a courier partner is assigned and pickup takes place within 2 business days. Please keep the item packed in its original box with the return label or order number clearly written on the outer carton.

## Refund Processing and Limits

Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product reaches our warehouse or after an instant refund is issued by support. Automated phone support can issue refunds up to ₹5,000 per order (and never exceeding the order total). Refunds above ₹5,000 require review and approval by a human support specialist; our agent will offer to transfer you to a human specialist or create a priority ticket.

## Store Credit Option

If your order is outside the 30-day return window but was delivered within the last 90 days, we can offer Kettle & Co store credit up to the full order total. Store credit is added immediately to your customer profile, never expires, and can be redeemed on any future purchase on our website.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your kitchenware arrives with transit damage, dents, chipped enamel, or missing parts, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange an immediate replacement, repair under warranty, or a full refund to your original payment method at no cost to you.
